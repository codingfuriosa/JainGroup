Overview

Designed to elevate every stay

Jain Group's portfolio of ground-breaking developments is carefully designed to enhance residents' and visitors' experiences — with clubhouses, state-of-the-art fitness and leisure facilities, restaurants, hotels and serviced apartments that meet the growing need for convenient business and leisure accommodation.

In partnership with IHG

Holiday Inn, Kolkata

A joint venture with IHG Hotels delivering sustainable living for everyone, located 5 km from Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport. Streamlined rooms feature free Wi-Fi, flat-screen TVs, desks, mini-fridges, minibars and tea/coffee facilities, with 24×7 room service.

Amenities include a colourful all-day restaurant, a bright patisserie, a laid-back grill-style bar, a gym and an outdoor swimming pool.

Business & Leisure

Sarovar Portico, New Town

A contemporary business and leisure hotel opposite Eco Park, minutes from the city's commercial hub. Its 128 well-appointed rooms and suites blend warm hospitality with refined convenience — high-speed Wi-Fi, 24-hour room service and elegant interiors crafted for work and relaxation.

Signature all-day diner 'Golpo' serves global and Indian cuisine, while the 'Conclave' boardroom is ideal for meetings. Just 11 km from Kolkata International Airport, near Mother's Wax Museum and Rabindra Tirtha.
