About the Company

A seasoned name across three industries.

The Jain Group is one of the most dynamic and admired organisations in the Real Estate, Hospitality and Finance sectors in East India. More than three decades ago, the visionary Founder & CMD, Prem Jain, established the auto-financing division — Jain Finance Corporation — earning recognition as a seasoned player in automobile finance.

Topaz Motors came into being in 1998 as a car dealership, and within two years bagged the Best Dealer Award for Eastern India, going on to become a Skoda Auto India dealer. The Group then ventured into Real Estate, creating residential spaces across Kolkata, Siliguri and Durgapur under the Dream Homes brand.

Having developed over a million square feet of residential and commercial real estate, the Group has diversified into 5-star hospitality through a strategic alliance with IHG and its Holiday Inn brand — committed to shaping the future of cities across the country.

Mr. Shrayans Jain

Vice Chairman, Jain Group

A commerce graduate from Kolkata who studied contemporary architecture and monument studies, Mr. Jain christened the Group's real estate division 'Dream Developers' in the early 2000s. Educated at St. Paul's, Darjeeling, he is a regular contributor to dailies like the Times of India and has featured on Zee TV for his real-estate acumen.

His foresight has helped the organisation grow in geometric progression and branch into Hospitality and IT-enabled services across India. Under his leadership the company has grown over five-fold in five years.

Mr. Rishi Jain

Executive Director, Jain Group

A commerce graduate from Kolkata with a post-graduation from S.P. Jain Institute of Management and Research, Mumbai, Mr. Jain completed Family Business Management at the Indian School of Business, Hyderabad, and trained in Finance with the ICAI.

Entrusted with developing the contemporary corporate aspects of a rapidly advancing Group, he has driven outstanding achievements and future potential across Real Estate, Hospitality and IT-enabled services.

Growth & Presence

Well-diversified, steadily growing.

The Jain Group has operated for more than half a century and is well-diversified across Finance, Real Estate and Hospitality. As it has grown from strength to strength, it has consolidated its market share in each vertical.

Headquartered in Kolkata, the Group's presence spans Jharkhand, Bihar and Odisha, and major cities including Jamshedpur, Durgapur and Siliguri. Its residential properties in Durgapur, Siliguri and Kolkata perform strongly in both sales and trust, with steadily rising revenue from its diversified businesses.
