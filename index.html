Our Story

Three decades of building dreams in Eastern India.

The Jain Group is one of the most dynamic and admired organisations in the Real Estate, Hospitality and Finance sectors in East India. What began with the visionary Founder & CMD Prem Jain has grown into a trusted name behind the Dream Homes brand.

Having developed over a million square feet of residential and commercial space, the Group continues to launch projects across Kolkata, Siliguri and Durgapur — built on a foundation of comfort, luxury and uncompromising quality.

Buyer FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about buying a flat or apartment with the Jain Group in Kolkata, Siliguri and Durgapur.

Which residential projects does the Jain Group offer in Kolkata?
Ready-to-move and new-launch projects across Kolkata, Siliguri and Durgapur — including Dream One, Dream World City, Dream Gurukul, Dream Eco City and Dream Valley — offering 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK flats.
Does the Jain Group have ready-to-move flats?
Yes. Dream One, Dream World City and Dream Eco City offer ready-to-move 2 and 3 BHK apartments, while Dream Gurukul is a new-launch project with possession in December 2027.
What flat configurations (BHK) are available?
From compact 1 BHK to spacious 4 BHK — for example, Dream World City offers 1–3 BHK and Dream One offers 2–4 BHK. See each project on the Our Development page.
What is the price range of Jain Group apartments?
Prices start from around ₹27.5 lacs (Dream World City) and go up to ₹98 lacs onwards (Dream One), depending on the project, location and configuration. Contact our team for current pricing.
Which cities does the Jain Group build in?
Kolkata, Siliguri and Durgapur — backed by 50+ years of experience, 4,000+ happy families and over 2.5 million sq. ft. delivered.
Are Jain Group projects RERA-compliant?
Yes. Our projects are developed in compliance with applicable RERA regulations. Please ask our sales team for the RERA registration number of a specific project.
Can I book a site visit?
Absolutely. Contact us or message us on WhatsApp to schedule a guided site visit at any project.
Does the Jain Group help with home loans?
Yes. Our team can help coordinate home-loan assistance with leading banks and guide you through the documentation and approval process.
Is there a referral or rewards programme?
Yes — refer your friends and family to a Jain Group residence and earn attractive rewards through our referral programme.
Why buy a home from the Jain Group?
Over five decades of trust, 4,000+ families settled, award-winning quality (CRISIL, CNBC, Realty+) and global tie-ups like Versace and Hager — backed by a dedicated community-management team. See our Wall of Honour.
